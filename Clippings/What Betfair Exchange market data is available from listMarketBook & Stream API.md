See examples & screenshots below which represent the specific prices/data that will be included in any updates when you subscribe to or request them via the **[Exchange Stream API](https://docs.developer.betfair.com/display/1smk3cen4v3lu3yomq5qye0ni/Exchange+Stream+API#ExchangeStreamAPI-Marketdatafieldfiltering/MarketDataFilter)** or **[listMarketBook](https://docs.developer.betfair.com/display/1smk3cen4v3lu3yomq5qye0ni/listMarketBook)**/listRunnerBook. 

**The screenshot shows the equivalent data on the Betfair Exchange website**.

Virtual prices are calculated for all ladder levels.                       | **This is the best available back and lay price available as displayed on the Betfair Exchange website**  ![mceclip0.png](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/article_attachments/6677774149533)                                                                  |
| EX\_BEST\_OFFERS                     | EX\_BEST\_OFFERS               | Best prices not including [Virtual Bets](https://docs.developer.betfair.com/display/1smk3cen4v3lu3yomq5qye0ni/Exchange+Stream+API) - depth is controlled by ladderLevels (1 to 10)                                                                                                                                                                                                   | **See above, but this will be without** [**Virtual Bets**](https://docs.developer.betfair.com/display/1smk3cen4v3lu3yomq5qye0ni/Exchange+Stream+API) **which are provided via default via the Betfair website. Virtual bets are explained in the documented article.** |
