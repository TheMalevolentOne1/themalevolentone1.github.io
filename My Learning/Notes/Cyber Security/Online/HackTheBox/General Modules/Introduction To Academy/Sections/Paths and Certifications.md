# What is a Path?
**Paths** are a collection of modules that share a broader topic. Certain paths lead to an **HTB Certification** after completing their corresponding **exam**. A single module may show up in multiple paths if its topic matches the path's topic.
## Types of Paths
There are two types of paths in HTB Academy: **Skill Paths** and **Job-Role Paths**. You can enrol in any path by clicking on the **Enrol** button, which will make it easier to track the progress in the path and see the upcoming modules in it through the Dashboard page.

Note: Enrolling in a path does not unlock all of the modules within it. Hence enrolling in a path does not cost any cubes.

A **Skill Path** groups modules within a specific popular skill that students may want to learn. For example, students who are looking to learn the basics of different operating systems can enrol in the **Operating System Fundamentals** path.

`Image of module example from modules list`

**Job-Role Paths** on the other hand are curated to prepare students for a specific job role in the cyber security market. Each Job-Role Path is linked to an HTB Certification, which requires passing a multi-day practical exam to obtain.

`Image of Job Role Path example`

Note: Estimate how long a path will take to complete by checking the direction mentioned underneath the path's title. This represents the sum of the **estimated time for completion** of each module in the path *in 8-hour work days*.

# Obtaining a Certification
An HTB Certification is the key achievement students should target within HTB Academy. As earlier mentioned, each certification is linked to a Job-Role path, which is specifically designed to prepare the student for the certification exam.

`Images of different HTB certifications`

Any student of HTB Academy, regardless of their level, can progress through the academy and follow a path to advance their skills until they obtain a certification.

By the time a certification is obtained, hundreds of sections and exercises, tens of modules and skills assessments and have passed a real-world multi-day practical exam. So the user will be **market-ready** allegedly to HTB for the specific job role.

Note: Completing a path unlocks a new badge.
