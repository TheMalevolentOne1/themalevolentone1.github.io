# How to proceed when stuck
During the learning journey, the user may face hurdles and get stuck on specific challenges, however there are ways in the HTB Academy to get support in learning.

For general questions about certain features within the academy by clicking on the **help button** found in the button-right corner of the screen.

`image of lower corner with support button and support page`

If struggling with exercises or skills assessments then the academy also provides detailed solutions for every exercise with a fully guided learning mode.

Simply clicking the **Show Solution** button found underneath HTB exercises, the available solution will be available. _This Feature is only available to HTB annual subscription plan and business customers_.

It is recommended to join the HTB community discord server, sub-reddit or forum to ask questions and help others along their cyber security journey.
