HTB Academy materials are composed of digestible chunks to enable seamless understanding of the content. The largest learning block available is called a path, which is broken up into multiple modules which cover a specific self contained topic. Further modules are broken down into their own unique sections.

`Insert Image Here of Path Image Thing.`

Some paths are linked to specific certifications which can be obtained after completing the path and exam.

While a major step like a path, may initially seem intimidating and heavy with information, the dissection of the paths into modules into sections makes it easier to learn as a result and allows anyone to start with a single sections until they reach the overarching achievement.

`Insert Mountain of HTB Climbing image.`

This process not only allows you to take it one step at a time but also ensures you are constantly testing your knowledge, to define and challenge your skills necessary to gain reaffirm the knowledge provided through the modules.
