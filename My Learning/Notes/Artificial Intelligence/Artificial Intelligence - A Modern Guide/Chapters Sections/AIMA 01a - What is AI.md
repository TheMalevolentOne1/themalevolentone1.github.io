# 1.1 What is AI?
>[!QUOTE]
>Some have defined intelligence in terms of *fidelity* in regards to human performance, (i.e. that ai should behave as closely to humans as possible) while others prefer an abstract formal definition of intelligence called *rationality* - doing the "right thing".
>
## What is Intelligence?
Intelligence is considered from two varying ideas:
- That intelligence stems from the internal thoughts and reasoning 
**or**
- The idea of intelligent behaviour and "external characterisation"

>[!Important]
> Machine-Learning is a **sub-field** of artificial intelligence which studies the ability to improve performance based on experience.  
> Some AI models use Machine-Learning and some do not.

AI is in the pursuit of human-like intelligence and it is believed that it must be in part an *empirical science* related to psychology, involving observations and predictions regarding human behaviour, thought processes and actions, and that with a rational approach involve a combination of mathematics, engineering and statistics.
