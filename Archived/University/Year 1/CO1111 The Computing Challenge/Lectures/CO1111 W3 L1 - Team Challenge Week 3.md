[[CO1111 The Computing Challenge]]
[[CO1111 W3 Lecture 1 - Symposium and Poster]]

Your **Team’s Main Challenge** this week is focused on finishing your **app** and getting ready for the **symposium**.

**Requirements:**

A fully functional and tested **app** capable of completing the Quest (see below updated requirements)

An eye catching **poster** that includes all of the required information

An engaging **stall** that shows off your teams achievements in their best light 

  

**Additional App Requirements:**

1. App must be capable of starting a quest using a **category** other than the default e.g. jedw.co.uk/challenge/quest/startquest/playername/**testing.** You may use "_testing_" and "_compsci_" as examples for testing purposes. You will be given the name of a secret category on Friday so this needs to be able to be input via your app.
2. App must be capable of showing a **LONG** riddle upto 280 chars. 
3. App should be able to handle what happens when the quest ends and not simple display null once the last riddle is passed.
4. App must allow user to explicitely **complete** the quest e.g. call the **endquest** function jedw.co.uk/challenge/quest/**endquest**. Otherwise the game attempt will not be logged.
