[[CO1111 The Computing Challenge]]
[[CO1111 W2 Lecture 1 - Building The Quest]]

Your **Team’s Main Challenge** this week is to continue developing your app and produce your teams marketing strategy

The app and strategy will be presented on Friday morning at The Friday Showcase.

**App Requirements:**

The app should connect to the server and begin a quest

The app should receive and display riddles from the server

The app should submit solutions to riddles to the server

The app should be able to advance to the next riddle (also from the server)

The app should be designed in accordance with guidelines with consideration to good user experience

**Marketing Requirements:**

Produce a (potentially) viral video to be shown at the Friday Showcase

Produce a marketing strategy including: logo, colour scheme, identity, unique selling points etc.
