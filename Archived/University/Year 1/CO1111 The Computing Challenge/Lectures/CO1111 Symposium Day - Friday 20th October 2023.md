[[CO1111 The Computing Challenge]]

**Symposium Day**

**Congratulations on reaching Symposium Day!**

This is a very special day, where you get to show off all your hard work from the last three weeks, review what everyone else has achieved, and enjoy your celebrations, as well as evaluate your team's efforts.

The itinerary for the day:

**8:30-12am - The Symposium - Foster Social Space: arrive early to set up your stalls!**

During the symposium:

- As a team you should judge 3 other stalls. When you have judged a stall leave behind a post-it note with your team code on to show how many times each stall has been visited.
- Individually (for your assignment) you should make a note of your favourite stalls and apps, and why they are your favourite. You should take photos of these.

**1:00pm - The Quest - Begins in the** **Mitchell and Kenyon Cinema (Foster Building):** 

- Time to test your apps out for real!
- You will have two hours to complete **The Quest**. 
- Compete for glory and ascend to the heights of the leaderboard (and maybe win a prize)!

**4pm - The Finale - Foster Social Space**

A celebration of all your hard work, and the crowning of the winning team.

We hope you have enjoyed the last few weeks - you have achieved a lot in such a short time.

You have:

- Made a network of professional associates - friends are a very important part of success.
- Made an impression on your tutors and got to know us better.
- Completed an end-to-end development project.
- Worked as part of a development team.
- Developed many transferable skills, e.g. communicating in many forms, presenting, collaborative working, etc.
- Had a taster of what is to come: Agile development practices, Programming, Storing and manipulating data, Algorithmic complexity, Network communication protocols, Usability and functional testing, Security.

**There is such a lot to be proud of - Well Done!!!**
