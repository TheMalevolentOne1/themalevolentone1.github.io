[[CO1111 The Computing Challenge]]
[[CO1111 W3 Lecture 2 - Testing and Poster Feedback]]

In today's lab you will be taking the test.

**Rules:**
 • Your **phone** must be turned off 

 • You must not speak to or communicate in any way with anyone other than your tutor

 • You do not require any equipment to complete the test but you may take blank paper and a pen

 • If you have a question during the test please raise your hand

 • Anyone suspected of cheating during the test will be cautioned and may be awarded 0 marks

 • No-one will be allowed to begin the test if they arrive more than 10 minutes late

 • If you **finish** the test early, **please leave the room quietly** and take a break - **return once the hour has elapsed**

 • If you wish to visit the toilet, please raise your hand and notify the tutor where you are going

In the **second hour** you will have an opportunity to continue working on your **poster** with **feedback** from the tutor.
