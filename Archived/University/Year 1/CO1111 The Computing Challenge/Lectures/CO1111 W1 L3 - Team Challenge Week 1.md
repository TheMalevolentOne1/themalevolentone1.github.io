[[CO1111 The Computing Challenge]]
[[CO1111 W1 Lecture 3 - Introduction to APIs]]

Your **Team’s Main Challenge** this week is to build a prototype ‘Riddle’ app.

This will form the basis of your Quest app that you will build over the next 2 weeks. The prototype will be demonstrated on Friday morning at The Friday Showcase.

**Prototype Requirements:**
The app should present the user with a series of riddles to be solved (minimum of 3 for demo purposes).

The user should be able to enter an answer and proceed to the next riddle.

The riddles should be presented one at a time, in a fixed order.

The app should show the user their score (based on the number of correct answers) at the end of the quest.

**Minimum Feature-Set:**
- Facility to enter player name
- Facility to display riddles
- Facility to submit riddle answers
- Facility to display the leaderboard (from labsheet3)
