# Module Aims
- To develop an understanding of how **modern mobile development platforms** and the **skills required** to develop cross platform applications for mobile devices.
- To explore a range of technical problems and solutions inherent in developing a cross platform applications for ubiquitous computing platforms.
- To establish an understanding of the key challenges in designing useful, usable, and desirable mobile applications and learn design patterns, heuristics and techniques to address them.
- To build an understanding of the unique features of modern cross platform development and how they can be used in interactive applications.

# Why Mobile
Mobile computing devices today outnumber computers worldwide. Since the early 2000s the exponential rise in the mobile device ownership has provided an, ever-present, always on, anytime, anywhere connected to the internet, simplistic ability to make and receive phone calls, play games, and run applications and services
