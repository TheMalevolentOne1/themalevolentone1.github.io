# Notice
First of all don't panic. Speak with me if you have any questions. Otherwise, use the below to help you understand what is required.

Results have be published (to your MyUCLan area) on **Monday 16th June**. On this day, you will be able to see your grades for each module and also your overall recommendation (which is likely to be one of: 'proceed', 'referred', 'deferred', or 'fail' (there are a few different fail options). If you have been given a recommendation of 'refer' or 'defer', this means that you should do the equivalent assessment over the summer.

Any coursework would be due to be submitted on or before **17th July 12:00pm**, and any exams would be scheduled for the week **Semester 3** (**4th to 15th August 2025)**. You would only be offered repair work for a failed module (so if one of your assessments has a high mark, it is possible to pass the module overall, even though you have failed one of the assessments). If it is a 'refer', the work will be capped at 40%, if it is a 'defer' the work is not capped. It is usually just the piece of work that is capped, not the whole module. If you think you have failed this (or any) module due to a low grade or non-submission of the coursework, you can make a start on the repair work now - before results are published. We are offering the work early so that you can plan the work and spread the workload over a longer period, especially if you have failed a number of modules. Be aware that if you pass the module, you will not be required to submit it, but you will have strengthened what you have learned, so it won't be wasted.

If you have not passed this module (or any other module), you will get a Teams call on (or shortly after) results day from a member of the Team to discuss what you need to do. If you don't take the call, we will leave you a voicemail message, which Teams automatically transcribes and sends as an email.

**Read this carefully if you did not submit the coursework for CO2404, or have a grade of less than 40%.**

The repair work is for anyone who did not submit or achieved less than 40% for the original submission. Note that if you did well in the exam, you may have passed the module overall already, in which case you are not required to do this work. The repair work for this module will be the same as the original set for assessment 1. Submit your work using the "Assignment 1 Resubmission" link in the "Assignments" tab. The absolute deadline for completing this work is **12.00pm** on **17th July** and you are expected to capture a video of your demo and submit a URL along with your submission - in the video you should walk though how your application meets the learning objectives and the marking criteria. (You should use Microsoft Stream and make sure I have permission to view it).
