# Lab Tasks
> [!TASK] Dataset Analysis Task
**Please refer to the example 'Datasets' and examine each dataset in turn. Consider the following:**
>1. List two or three questions that could be proposed to provide insights/predictions.
>2. Based on these questions, what features do you deem as important for the analysis?
>3. Are there potentially missing features?
>4. How do you know the data is valid?
>5. How could bias be minimised if appropriate?
>6. What distributions are noticeable?
>7. Are there any data points that look out of place.
