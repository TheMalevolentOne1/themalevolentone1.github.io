# Brief Instructions
>[!pdf] Key
>Orange - In reference of work required.
>Red - In reference to supervisor actions.

> [!PDF|orange] [[CO3008 Assignment Brief 2026 Final.pdf#page=1&selection=22,0,29,53&color=orange|CO3008 Assignment Brief 2026 Final, p.1]]
> > In this assessment you are to complete a substantial project showcasing the practical skills you have developed during your time as a student on your honours degree course. You will work individually to research and undertake practical work relevant to your course to help solve a clearly defined problem chosen by you. The results of your practical work normally result in the production of some kind of computational artefact. In addition to producing your artefact, you will document your planning, research, design, implementation and evaluation in a substantial report
> 

> [!PDF|red] [[CO3008 Assignment Brief 2026 Final.pdf#page=1&selection=29,55,31,70&color=red|CO3008 Assignment Brief 2026 Final, p.1]]
> > You will complete the project under the guidance of a supervisor who will meet with you throughout the duration of the project and guide you in the process of managing and completing a project of this size.
